AUTHORITY: Dhaka North City Corporation (DNCC)

CATEGORY: Road / Pothole

EVIDENCE:
Dhaka North City Corporation carries out road construction and road repair work within its jurisdiction. Its Annual Administrative Report 2024-25 describes construction and improvement of roads, drains and footpaths.

DNCC Annual Administrative Report FY 2024-25.
