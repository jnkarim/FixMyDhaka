AUTHORITY: Dhaka South City Corporation (DSCC)

CATEGORY: Road / Pothole

EVIDENCE:
Dhaka South City Corporation is responsible for construction and maintenance of roads, footpaths and drains within its jurisdiction. Its Engineering Department handles infrastructure development and repair activities.

SOURCE:
DSCC official Engineering Department and official city functions pages.


CATEGORY: Streetlight

EVIDENCE:
DSCC is responsible for public street lighting within its jurisdiction. Its official service page provides a central control room for streetlight related information and service.

SOURCE:
DSCC official Street Light service page.


CATEGORY: Garbage

EVIDENCE:
DSCC Waste Management Department collects solid waste from households, businesses, hospitals, streets, public toilets and drains. It also carries out road cleaning and waste management activities.

SOURCE:
DSCC official Waste Management Department page.


CATEGORY: Waterlogging / Drainage

EVIDENCE:
DSCC includes waterlogging mitigation and the construction, maintenance and improvement of drainage systems among its city functions. Its Engineering Department is responsible for developing and maintaining adequate drainage infrastructure.

SOURCE:
DSCC official city functions and Engineering Department pages.
